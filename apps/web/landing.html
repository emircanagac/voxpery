Voxpery

Text chat, voice channels, screen sharing, and moderation for your community. Free, open source, and available in your browser or on your own server.

  • Text channels, direct messages, voice, camera, and screen sharing in one app.
  • Use it in the browser, install it as a web app on your phone, or download it for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • No paid tier, no ads, and no analytics cookies.
  • All of the code is public under AGPL-3.0; run the same stack on your own server with Docker.
